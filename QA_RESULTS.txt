Scope / safety
- No vendor APIs, backend routes, database tables, Python dependencies, extensions or separate services added.
- History uses localStorage in each analyst's browser profile. It is not uploaded, shared, centrally backed up or synchronized between devices.
- JSON and CSV backups are available for manual transfer between browser profiles.
- Duplicate detection is scoped to the same vendor + normalized URL. Repeats are blocked by default and can be allowed explicitly by the analyst.
- Statuses are prepared/pending, portal opened, confirmed by analyst, and cancelled/discarded.
- A manual confirmation is an analyst assertion only; the app cannot verify that a vendor accepted or processed the submission.
- Vendor templates are guidance text. The analyst must review and submit each item in the official portal, including any authentication, CAPTCHA or additional fields.
- Imported records are sanitized; portal links are resolved from the built-in vendor allowlist, not trusted from the imported file.
- CSV export protects cells beginning with formula-trigger characters to reduce spreadsheet formula injection risk.

Warnings
- Two pre-existing FastAPI `on_event` deprecation warnings remain in the backend; this release does not alter startup lifecycle handling.
- Browser local storage can be deleted by clearing site data and may be subject to browser quotas. Export backups regularly. Large histories may be trimmed to fit local storage.
- Vendor forms, availability, URLs, login requirements, CAPTCHA and policies can change. This release does not test live vendor submissions.
